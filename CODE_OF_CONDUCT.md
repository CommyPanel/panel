# Code of Conduct

CommyPanel is built in the open by people with very different backgrounds and experience. We want everyone who takes part to feel welcome and to be able to do their best work.

## Our standard

Be kind, be patient and be constructive.

**What we encourage**

- Being respectful of other people's time, skills and opinions, including when you disagree.
- Giving feedback on the work, not the person, and saying what would make it better.
- Assuming good faith, and asking a question before assuming a mistake.
- Welcoming newcomers and answering "basic" questions without making anyone feel small.
- Saying so when you do not know something, and when you were wrong.

**What we do not accept**

- Harassment, insults, personal attacks or demeaning comments, in any form and about anything, including a person's identity, background, appearance or beliefs.
- Sexualised language or imagery, and unwelcome attention.
- Trolling, deliberately derailing a discussion, or repeatedly pushing after a decision has been explained.
- Publishing other people's private information, such as an address or an email, without their permission.
- Anything else that would reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Where this applies

In every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, and any place where you are representing the project in public.

## Enforcement

The maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing this standard. They may edit or remove comments, commits, issues and other contributions that do not follow it, and may warn, temporarily block or permanently ban anyone whose behaviour they consider inappropriate, threatening or harmful. They will explain the reason when it is safe to do so.

**To report a problem**, use the repository's private reporting form (Security, then "Report a vulnerability") and start the title with "Conduct:", or contact the repository owner through their GitHub profile. Reports are handled privately, and the privacy and safety of the person reporting comes first. Please do not report conduct problems in a public issue.
