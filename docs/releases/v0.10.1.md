# CommyPanel 0.10.1

A tidier app page, on top of 0.10.0.

## What's new

- **A shorter menu on the app page.** The twelve tabs are now five entries, with related sections together under a drop-down:
    - **Deployments**
    - **Runtime**: Usage, Logs, Terminal
    - **Networking**: Domains & SSL, Nginx, Links
    - **Configuration**: Environment, Volumes, Safe deploys, Settings
    - **Advanced**

    A drop-down is highlighted while one of its sections is open, closes with Escape or a click elsewhere, and can be used with the arrow keys. Sections you have no permission for are left out, and the counts for domains, variables and volumes sit next to their names. Addresses with `?tab=` keep working, and the warning about unsaved nginx changes still appears when you leave that section.

## Before you upgrade

- No new columns, no new permissions and no new ports. Run `sudo commypanel upgrade`.

## Known limitations

- Everything in 0.10.0 is unchanged.

## Requirements

Unchanged: Ubuntu 24.04 or 26.04, or Debian 13 or newer, with systemd; `amd64` or `arm64`; root access; at least 1 GB of RAM.

```bash
# Install
curl -fsSL https://github.com/commypanel/panel/releases/latest/download/install.sh | sudo bash

# Upgrade
sudo commypanel upgrade
```
